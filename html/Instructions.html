The Siege of Hexfall

A cooperative heroes-vs-the-game board game

2. Objective

Heroes must work together to seal all Portals and defeat the Boss.

If all Heroes are defeated or two Villages are destroyed, the game is lost.

4. Setup

  1. Each player selects one Hero. If playing with fewer than four players, distribute unused Heroes so each player controls an equal number.
  2. All Heroes begin on Village tiles.

7. Hero Phase

7.2 Sealing a Portal

Any Hero standing on a Portal tile may attempt to seal it.

  • Without a Portal Shard: Failure deals 5 damage.
  • With a Portal Shard: Failure deals 2 damage.

A Hero may carry only one Portal Shard at a time.

7.3 Loot a Ruin

Each Ruin may be looted only once. The Portal Shard is the Ruin’s loot.

  • Ruin bonuses persist for the rest of the game.
  • Health bonuses do not increase a Hero above their maximum Health.

8. Monster Phase

Target Priority

  1. Closest Hero
  2. Closest Village

If a Monster is on a Village tile and a Hero is one tile away, the Monster attacks the Village.

If a Hero and Village are the same distance, the Monster targets the Hero.

Village Attacks

Monster attacks against Villages automatically hit and do not require a die roll.

10. Hero Defeat

  • When a Hero is defeated, any Portal Shard they were carrying is dropped on their tile.
  • Dropped Shards may be picked up by other Heroes.

13. End of Game

The Heroes win only if all Portals are sealed and the Boss is defeated.
